## 2. Definitions

Terms used in this DPA have the meaning given in Regulation (EU) 2016/679
("**GDPR**") unless otherwise defined here.

- "**Personal Data**" means personal data, as defined in Article 4(1)
  GDPR, that WiseGift processes on behalf of the Merchant under the Main
  Agreement.
- "**Data Subject**" means the natural persons to whom the Personal Data
  relates — for the purposes of this DPA, visitors to the Merchant's
  online storefront who interact with the WiseGift widget.
- "**Sub-processor**" means any third party engaged by WiseGift to
  process Personal Data on WiseGift's behalf.
- "**Services**" means the WiseGift recommendation infrastructure
  described in the Main Agreement, comprising the embeddable widget, the
  merchant admin dashboard, and the recommendation and event APIs.

## 3. Roles of the Parties

The Merchant is the **Controller** of Personal Data relating to visitors
to its storefront. The Merchant determines the purposes and means of the
processing through its decision to install and configure the Services on
its storefront.

WiseGift is a **Processor** acting on the documented instructions of the
Merchant. WiseGift does not determine the purposes of the processing and
does not process Personal Data for its own purposes.

## 4. Subject matter and duration

**4.1 Subject matter.** WiseGift processes Personal Data solely to
provide the Services to the Merchant: serving product recommendations
to storefront visitors, measuring the effectiveness of those
recommendations against a 10% holdout, and enforcing per-tenant cost
guardrails.

**4.2 Duration.** Processing begins on the date the Merchant installs the
WiseGift application on its storefront and continues until the
Merchant uninstalls the application, subject to the 30-day soft-delete
window described in clause 12.

## 5. Categories of Data Subjects

Visitors to the Merchant's online storefront who render the WiseGift
widget in their browser or whose orders are received by WiseGift via
the Merchant's e-commerce platform webhook.

## 6. Categories of Personal Data

WiseGift's data collection is deliberately narrow. The categories of
Personal Data processed under this DPA are limited to:

**6.1 Anonymous session data**, generated by the widget in the visitor's
browser:

- an anonymous session identifier (UUID v4, stored in the visitor's
  browser under a WiseGift-scoped `localStorage` key with a 30-day
  sliding time-to-live), and
- a deterministic exposed-vs-holdout assignment derived from that
  identifier.

**6.2 Widget interaction events**, tagged with the anonymous session
identifier:

- event type (`widget_shown`, `widget_engaged`, `intent_submitted`,
  `product_clicked`),
- placement, intent mode (self / gift), holdout flag, timestamp,
- for intent submissions: the visitor's selected recipient relationship
  (Partner / Family / Friend / Colleague / Other), occasion (from a
  fixed list), budget band, and optional free-text interest chips
  (maximum 5),
- for product clicks: the platform product identifier of the clicked
  recommendation.

**6.3 Filtered order data**, received from the Merchant's e-commerce
platform via webhook and filtered at ingest:

- order identifier, line items (platform product identifier, quantity,
  price), total price, currency, timestamp, and (where the widget
  planted a session correlator) the anonymous session identifier.

**6.4 Categories of data that are NEVER processed.** WiseGift never
receives, requests, or stores:

- the visitor's name, e-mail address, postal address, telephone
  number, IP address, or user-agent string;
- the visitor's payment or financial data;
- the Merchant's customer database or any join key against it;
- any cross-storefront browsing behaviour or third-party profile
  enrichment.

Customer identity fields present on the raw order webhook payload
(`customer`, `billing_address`, `shipping_address`, `email`, `phone`,
`client_details`) are dropped at the receiver, before any log line is
written and before any data is persisted.

## 7. Purposes and nature of processing

**7.1 Purposes.** WiseGift processes Personal Data solely to:

- (a) render personalised product recommendations to storefront visitors
  in response to widget interactions;
- (b) compute per-Merchant lift metrics against a 10% holdout
  (widget click-through rate, add-to-cart rate on recommended
  products, conversion rate, average order value, revenue per
  session);
- (c) enforce per-tenant cost guardrails, including monthly usage caps,
  per-session rate limits, response caching, and the per-tenant
  kill switch.

**7.2 Nature of processing.** Processing consists of:

- short-lived per-request inference calls against large-language-model
  APIs (Anthropic Claude) to re-rank candidate products against the
  visitor's submitted intent;
- retrieval from a per-tenant vector index computed at catalog ingest;
- cached responses in an in-region cache with a 24-hour time-to-live;
- durable storage of session, event, and filtered-order rows in a
  per-tenant-scoped Postgres database.

## 8. Merchant instructions

**8.1 Documented instructions.** The Merchant instructs WiseGift to

**8.2 Compliance with instructions.** WiseGift processes Personal Data

## 10. International transfers

**10.1 EU-only at MVP.** WiseGift's production storage and compute
infrastructure is located exclusively in the European Union at the
Effective Date. This includes primary database, cache, backend
hosting, and observability. No transfer of Personal Data outside the
EU/EEA takes place from these components.

**10.2 Sub-processors involving transfers.** Certain Sub-processors
listed in the Sub-processor List (specifically, the LLM inference
provider and the embedding provider) are established in the United
States. Any transfer of Personal Data to those Sub-processors relies
on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 3 —
Processor to Processor) executed between WiseGift and each such
Sub-processor, together with the supplementary measures described in
clause 11. As set out in the Sub-processor List, product catalog data
(not shopper session data) is sent to the embedding provider, and
only intent payloads (not session identifiers or shopper-identifying
data) are sent to the LLM inference provider.

**10.3 Change of region.** If WiseGift adds a non-EU processing region
(for example a US region), WiseGift will notify the Merchant at least
30 days in advance and this DPA will be re-executed to reflect the
updated data-transfer posture.

## 11. Security measures (Article 32)

WiseGift implements the following technical and organisational measures,
which the Parties agree provide an appropriate level of security taking
into account the state of the art and the nature of the Personal Data:

**11.1 Multi-tenant isolation.** Every table containing Merchant data
carries a `tenant_id` column marked `NOT NULL`; every application
query is scoped to the calling tenant; a repository-layer test rejects
any query missing the tenant predicate. Cross-tenant reads are
architecturally prohibited.

**11.2 Encryption at rest and in transit.** All Personal Data is
encrypted at rest at the database layer (Neon-managed encryption) and
in transit over TLS 1.2 or higher on every network hop (widget → API,
admin → API, API → Sub-processor).

**11.3 Widget authentication.** The widget authenticates to the
WiseGift API using short-lived HMAC-signed request tokens (one-hour
time-to-live) bound to the Merchant's tenant identifier and
storefront-origin allowlist.

**11.4 Webhook authentication.** All e-commerce platform webhooks are
verified with HMAC signature checks against the platform's shared
secret; failed verifications are rejected with a 401 response and
logged without payload contents.

**11.5 Cost and abuse guardrails.** Per-tenant monthly usage cap,
per-session rate limit, and per-tenant kill switch are enforced at
the API layer to bound abuse and cost.

**11.6 PII filtering at ingest.** Customer identity fields on the raw
order webhook payload are dropped at the receiver, before any log
write and before persistence. This is enforced by code and covered
by integration tests.

**11.7 No logging of Personal Data or secrets.** Structured application
logs never include secrets, authentication tokens, or the Personal
Data described in clause 6. Error responses do not leak internal
state.

**11.8 Merchant admin authentication.** Merchant admin users
authenticate via e-mail and password (hashed with a modern
memory-hard function) or Google OAuth. Session cookies are HTTPOnly,
SameSite=Lax, and Secure.

**11.9 OAuth token storage.** E-commerce platform OAuth tokens are
encrypted at rest with a key managed in a dedicated secrets manager
and are decrypted in memory only for the outbound platform call.

**11.10 Personnel.** WiseGift personnel with access to production
systems are bound by written confidentiality obligations and receive
training on WiseGift's data-handling policies.

**11.11 Change control.** Production changes go through a code-review
and CI pipeline that runs unit, integration, and tenant-isolation
tests before deployment. Deployment to production is human-gated.

## 12. Data subject rights assistance

**12.1 Nature of the data set.** Because WiseGift does not process
direct identifiers of Data Subjects (clause 6.4), WiseGift is not in
a position to identify a specific Data Subject on request. WiseGift
will not attempt to re-identify Data Subjects.

**12.2 Assistance.** Where the Merchant receives a data subject
request (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability,
objection) and reasonably requires WiseGift's assistance, WiseGift
will provide reasonable cooperation on a best-effort basis and
respond to the Merchant's request within **30 days** of receipt.
Typical assistance is limited to confirming which categories of data
WiseGift holds and, where the Merchant can provide an anonymous
session identifier, deleting or exporting the associated rows.

## 13. Personal data breach notification

WiseGift will notify the Merchant without undue delay and in any event
within **72 hours** of becoming aware of a Personal Data breach
affecting Merchant data. The notification will include, to the extent
then known, the nature of the breach, categories and approximate
volume of Data Subjects and records affected, likely consequences,
measures taken or proposed, and a contact point for further
information.

## 14. Return or deletion of Personal Data

**14.1 On termination or uninstall.** Upon the Merchant uninstalling
the WiseGift application from its storefront (which triggers the
platform's uninstall webhook to WiseGift), the Merchant's tenant is
placed into a soft-deleted state. Widget rendering stops and catalog
sync is halted immediately.

**14.2 30-day soft-delete window.** For **30 days** after soft-delete,
the tenant's data is retained to permit reactivation on reinstall.
During this window no new processing occurs, but Personal Data
remains stored.

**14.3 Hard purge.** After the 30-day soft-delete window elapses, a
scheduled job hard-deletes all rows across every WiseGift database
table carrying that tenant's identifier, including catalog rows,
embeddings, sessions, events, filtered order rows, and cost
telemetry.

**14.4 Backups.** Personal Data may persist in encrypted database
backups for a period of up to 30 days after hard purge, after which
backups expire and are deleted. During that period the data is not
accessible to production systems.

**14.5 Export before termination.** The Merchant may request an export
of its data at any time before hard purge by contacting WiseGift
support. WiseGift will provide the export as a JSONL file via signed
URL within 5 business days.

## 15. Audit rights

**15.1 Information.** WiseGift will make available to the Merchant,
on written request no more than once per year, information reasonably
necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including its
security-measures documentation and its Sub-processor List.

**15.2 Audit.** Where the information above is not sufficient, the
Merchant may, at its own cost and on reasonable prior written notice,
conduct or mandate a qualified third-party auditor (subject to
confidentiality) to audit WiseGift's compliance with this DPA. Any
such audit will be conducted during business hours, without
disrupting WiseGift's operations, and no more than once per year
unless a Personal Data breach has occurred.
